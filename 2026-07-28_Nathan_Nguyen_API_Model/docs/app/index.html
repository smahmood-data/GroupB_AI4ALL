Puerto Rico health data ↗ Methodology
How the estimate is made

Three kinds of information shape the weekly estimate.

1
Reported cases

Official Puerto Rico reports show how dengue activity changed in earlier weeks.

2
Weather and season

Rain, temperature, humidity, wind, and the time of year help identify conditions linked with dengue.

3
A range, not one certainty

The app shows a starting estimate and a higher range because the future cannot be known exactly.
